### 14. ⚖️ THREE RULINGS FROM STAGE A's CLOSE (2026-08-03, TASK-478) — the falsified-assertion carve, the deferral-reason law, and the belt-to-only-strap clause

**⛔ (a) THE FALSIFIED-ASSERTION CARVE — §22 BEATS SINGLE-OWNER-PER-FILE, NARROWLY, AND TASK-478 WAS RIGHT NOT TO TAKE IT SILENTLY.**

- ⛔ **THE FOUR CONDITIONS — outside them, single-owner wins and the finding is ROUTED, not fixed:**
  1. **The string must assert something THIS task's own change made false.** ⛔ *"While I was in there"* is not this clause.

**⛔ (b) THE DEFERRAL-REASON LAW — A DEFERRAL CARRIES A REASON, AND THE REASON IS CHECKABLE.**
> ### ⛔ **A WRONG REASON ATTACHED TO A RIGHT DECISION IS HOW THE NEXT READER JUSTIFIES A WRONG ONE.**
