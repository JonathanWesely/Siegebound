### VER-§0. PURPOSE — WHAT A `VERIFIED` IS, AND ⛔ WHAT IT IS NOT
1. **The lane.** After a code row is `qa-passed` (text) and `built` (binaries exist), and only if its spec names a runtime-observable acceptance criterion, `playtest-verifier` maps each acceptance line to an observable, drives one PIE run through Aura's tools, and writes `.claude/pipeline/qa/TASK-###-verify.md` with `Verdict: VERIFIED | VERIFY-FAILED | UNOBSERVABLE` (| `MEASURED` since 2026-09-20, `VER-§1` cl. 1 / cl. 5a — added here 2026-09-26 by the manager on `TASK-1513`, `qa/TASK-1505.md` M5). Blueprint/asset-only rows enter from `qa-passed` directly (there is nothing to compile). The routing (5a compile → 5b verify → 5c commit) lives in `CLAUDE.md`; this section is the law those steps cite.
2. **A `VERIFIED` IS:** *"the engine, driven this way, did this, and here is the frame / the quoted widget value / the log line that shows it."* It is evidence gathered BEFORE 🧑 Jonathan's playtest so that his time is spent on judgment, not on discovering that a button does nothing.
3. **⛔ A `VERIFIED` IS NOT:** ⛔ not his playtest, ⛔ not a substitute for it, ⛔ not a `qa-passed` (the two verdicts are kept SEPARATE and both stay honest — *"the text says what it should"* vs *"the engine did what it should"*), ⛔ not a claim about anything the run did not observe, and ⛔ not a claim about taste (`SHIP-§8b(7)`: his eye wins). **`FR-§` ground truth stays:** a footage-review finding from HIS recording outranks a `VERIFIED` on the same behaviour — the verifier drove a scripted input; he played the game.
4. **The pixel-proof doctrine is inherited whole** (`FR-§3`, `SC-§20`): observations with evidence paths and quoted values, never conclusions; mechanism lines are HYPOTHESIS; a time-varying claim states its observation window in seconds (`SC-§115`).
5. **Until `VER-§6` is discharged the whole lane is ADVISORY** — see that section before treating any verdict as a gate.

### VER-§2. SERIALIZATION — ⛔ ONE VERIFICATION AT A TIME, AND ⛔ NEVER BESIDE A BUILD OR AN IMPORT
1. **Exactly one Aura verification is live across the whole pipeline at any moment.** The verifier drives the editor; two drivers on one editor is the same defect as two compile gates on one module (`THE QUIET-MODULE LAW`).
