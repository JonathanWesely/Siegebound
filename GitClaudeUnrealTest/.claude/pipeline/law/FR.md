### FR-§5. ⚠️ KNOWN LIMITATIONS — ⛔ NONE OF THEM ARE BUGS
- **No audio analysis** — anything audible-only is invisible; Jonathan's terminal description is the authoritative symptom source.
- **Temporal resolution is bounded by sampling** — coarse tiles are ±interval/2 (Game Bar captures are VFR); sub-interval events can be missed; `--run` is the instrument for suspected flicker.
- **Claude reads stills, not motion** — stutter/velocity judgments are frame-delta inferences and are labeled as such.
- **Capture compression can mimic rendering defects** — low-confidence findings are flagged, not asserted.
- **Sheet legibility is bounded by the model's image downscale** (~1568 px long edge); the 4x3 default was gate-verified with autocrop on 992×576 content — bigger content or subtler defects may need `--grid 3x2 --tile-width 640` or fine frames.
- ⛔⛔⭐⭐ **A ⛔ COLOUR-CENSUS COLUMN IS ⛔ NOT A STATE OBSERVATION — ⛔ VALIDATE ON ⛔ POSITIVE CONTROLS, THEN ⛔ READ THE FRAME BEFORE BELIEVING AN ⛔ EXCURSION** *(added 2026-09-03, from `VID-005` — ⛔ **a save, ⛔ not a failure**)*. ⛔ A whole-video tint census flagged **blue → 9.26 %**; ⛔ reading that frame showed the ⛔ newly-placed tower's ⛔ lit surfaces plus ⛔ two health bars, with ⛔ NO GHOST IN FRAME AT ALL. ⇒ ⛔⛔ **a naive read of that column would have ⛔ MANUFACTURED a false *"blue state observed"* — ⛔ a false positive that would have ⛔ CONTRADICTED THE TRUE FINDING and sent the fix ⛔ upstream to the ⛔ WRONG END.** ⭐ **What made it safe was ⛔ ordinary: the instrument was validated on ⛔ THREE positive controls ⛔ BEFORE it was trusted, and the ⛔ scene baseline (⛔ 0.1–9.3 % for blue) was ⛔ stated as a ⛔ range rather than assumed to be ⛔ zero.** ⚖️ *⛔ The conclusion survives ⛔ because the ⛔ negative finding rested on a ⛔ CONTRAST (⛔ blue flat at 1.16–1.66 while red swung to 58.2), ⛔ never on an ⛔ absolute threshold.*
- ⚠️⭐ **AN ⛔ ERROR MESSAGE IS A ⛔ MEASUREMENT, AND ⛔ *"NO SUCH FILE"* ⛔ OUTRANKS ⛔ *"EMPTY FOLDER"*** *(added 2026-09-03)*. ⛔ `FR-§1` ⛔ already pins the inbox at **`<git-root>/testvideo/`, ⛔ NOT the project dir** — ⛔ **the law was ⛔ right and a reader still ⛔ mis-pathed it.** ⭐ **The save was that the listing returned *"No such file or directory"* ⛔ rather than an ⛔ empty result** ⇒ ⛔ **it proved the ⛔ PATH was wrong instead of ⛔ reporting that the ⛔ FOLDER was empty.** ⇒ 📌 ⛔ **an instrument that ⛔ cannot distinguish *"absent"* from *"empty"* is ⛔ `SC-§39`'s defect wearing a ⛔ filesystem costume — ⛔ prefer the call that ⛔ errors.** ⛔ **Sibling exhibit, same batch: a binary probe used `strings`, which is ⛔ NOT INSTALLED here, and printed ⛔ empty for ⛔ ALL 8 FILES — ⛔ that zero WAS the instrument; ⛔ re-run with `grep -a` + ⛔ both controls.**
