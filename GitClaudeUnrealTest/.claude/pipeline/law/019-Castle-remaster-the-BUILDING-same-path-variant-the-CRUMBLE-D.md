- **⚠️ CRUMBLE-DERIVATION LAW (LOAD-BEARING — the trap this section exists for):** `SM_Castle_Crumble01/02/03` (`/Game/Meshes/`) are **byte-copy DUPLICATES of `SM_Castle`** (TASK-157) and `M_CastleCrumble` **samples the very same `T_Castle_{D,N,ORM}`**. A same-path Castle rebuild therefore hands the OLD crumble geometry a NEW UV layout ⇒ **the 75/50/25 % damage states render scrambled textures** unless they are re-derived. **Whenever `SM_Castle` geometry/UVs OR `T_Castle_*` change, `SM_Castle_Crumble01/02/03` MUST be re-derived by duplicating the REBUILT `SM_Castle` over them (same paths), and `MI_Castle_Crumble0N` re-assigned to BOTH slots of its stage mesh** (the TASK-157 recipe verbatim). The crumble MIs and `M_CastleCrumble` themselves are NOT re-authored — they pick the new albedo up for free through the same-path textures. Crumble MIs live at `/Game/Materials/` (NOT `Instances/`) — the `ACastle` code path is the contract and wins over the prefix-table folder row.
  - **WHY "BOTH SLOTS" IS LOAD-BEARING — verified in C++ at TASK-329, and it is the easy thing to get wrong.** `ACastle::ApplyCrumbleStage()` (`Castle.cpp:326`) does `CastleMesh->SetStaticMesh(CrumbleMesh); CastleMesh->SetMaterial(0, CrumbleMaterial);` — **slot 0 ONLY. Slot 1 is NEVER touched at runtime**, so it comes from the crumble asset's own saved material. A fresh duplicate of the rebuilt `SM_Castle` inherits slot 1 = `MI_Castle_PBR`, so unless slot 1 is **explicitly re-assigned at design time** the damaged castle renders **crumble material on the roofs and pristine castle material on the walls — a half-crumbled look, with NO error logged.** Slot 1 must be baked into the saved asset; there is no runtime path that will fix it.
  - **Evidence the crumbles really are stale byte-copies (read-only `.uasset` string inspection, TASK-329):** `SM_Castle_Crumble01` still carries **`Factory_RootNode.SM_Castle`** internally — proof it is a byte-copy of the OLD `SM_Castle` — and its slots are `[TeamRegion, CastlePBR]` with BOTH pre-assigned to `MI_Castle_Crumble01`. `M_CastleCrumble` samples `/Game/Textures/T_Castle_{D,N,ORM}` — the exact three textures a remaster overwrites. - **⚖️ ALBEDO ACCEPTANCE FLOOR — AMENDED 2026-07-27 (manager ruling on TASK-329). THE RAW ARM IS RETIRED AS A GATE; the UV-normalised arm is the sole albedo gate, for BUILDINGS AND UNITS ALIKE.** The clause previously read "≥ **0.164** raw **AND** ≥ **0.2536** UV-normalised". That dual form is withdrawn. **THE OPERATIVE FLOOR IS: mean linear albedo ≥ 0.2536 on the UV-NORMALISED (covered-texel) metric.** Raw all-pixel albedo is still MEASURED AND REPORTED on every asset for continuity with the audit record — it is **never a fail reason**. Four reasons, and they are settled; **do not re-litigate this on the next building rebuild**:
  1. **The raw arm carries no independent information — it is the UV-normalised metric multiplied by an atlas-packing variable.** `raw ≈ UV-normalised × UV-coverage`, confirmed to 4 dp across the measured set (Castle rebuilt `0.5847 × 0.192 = 0.1123`; Wall `0.2451 × 0.331 = 0.0811`; Footman `0.2536 × 0.646 = 0.1638`). Coverage is a function of **how the atlas was packed**, not of how the asset looks on screen: re-pack the same textures tighter and "raw" rises with zero visual change; add texel margin and it falls. Gating on raw gates on UV packing.
  2. **It is arithmetically unreachable for a building.** At the buildings' 12–33 % coverage, `raw ≥ 0.164` demands a covered-texel mean of ~0.50–1.37 (the Castle at 19.2 % coverage would need **0.854** — a near-white texture). **NO BUILDING HAS EVER REACHED IT**; the previous best was Wall at 0.0810, and the rebuilt Castle at 0.1123 is now the brightest building in the project by 39 %. Chasing the raw number means BLEACHING the asset — the exact defect this whole remaster exists to remove. TASK-329 correctly refused to chase it.
  3. **The reference asset itself fails it.** The Footman baseline measures **0.1639** against a 0.164 floor. A floor the baseline does not clear is a broken floor, not a high bar.
  4. **Assets Jonathan has already signed off fail it.** Ogre, Knight and Wall fail one or both arms as written.
  - **NOT RETROACTIVE.** This floor gates NEW and REBUILT assets. It does **not** re-open anything already shipped and signed off — in particular the eight audit-closed buildings stay closed (see the Fleet-Meshy-remaster building clause). Jonathan's eye remains the final acceptance authority over any measured number.
