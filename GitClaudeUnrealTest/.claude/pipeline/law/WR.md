### SC-§37 ⭐⭐ WHERE A VALUE'S CORRECTNESS IS INVISIBLE TO REVIEW, THE TEST MUST **MEASURE THE PROPERTY**, ⛔ NEVER RESTATE THE VALUE (added 2026-09-01; the pattern promoted from TASK-745)
