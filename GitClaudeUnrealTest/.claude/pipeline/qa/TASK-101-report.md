## Rulings on the 10 flagged decisions

1. **Nearest-unhit-to-previous bounce selection, ties by iteration order (strict `<`).** ACCEPTED. Ruling 9 pins the radius and origin but is silent on selection within the radius; nearest-first is the established AcquireTarget house pattern and is deterministic. 
2. **Bounce class gate = §3.7 units+hero only, via shared IsAcquirableEnemy (chains never hit buildings/castles).** ACCEPTED — this is the correct reading. GDD §4 defines Chain as "attack bounces to additional nearby targets" (class-silent); the chain IS the tower's attack, and tower target grammar is §3.7 "targets units/hero". Ruling 9 and the TASK-101 spec both say bounces go to "additional *enemies*" in a tower context where "enemy" has always meant the §3.7 set. Castle exclusion also matches ruling 4's anti-sniping intent (no chain-sniping the castle from a tower). Sharing the gate instead of mirroring it makes drift structurally impossible (qa/TASK-026 NIT-4 discipline). Carry-forward for manager: if design later wants chains jumping to enemy buildings, that is a rule change (one-line gate swap), not a bug.
3. **Two-phase snapshot-then-apply atomicity.** ACCEPTED. "INSTANT-hit" (ruling 9) reads as one atomic zap; interleaving damage with search would make chain shape depend on cascade-death timing. Null-safety verified: phase 2 `IsValid` guard (:431) skips torn-down actors entirely (no damage, no VFX); dead-but-not-destroyed actors are zeroed by receivers' own dead-gates (documented house contract). No dangling deref possible.
4. **Bounces may step outside the tower's 800 Range.** ACCEPTED — literal and correct reading of ruling 9: only the primary is Range-gated ("Primary = nearest valid enemy in Range (800)"); bounces are gated *solely* by ChainBounceRadius "of the PREVIOUS target". Adding a tower-range cap would be inventing a rule the manager didn't write. Carry-forward observation for the manager's balance backlog: worst-case chain reach is 800 + 2×350 = 1500 uu — beyond even Ballista's 1400 Range. Spec-conformant; playtest will judge.
5. **NS_ChainZap at EVERY hit regardless of damage outcome.** ACCEPTED. With no projectile actor, the zap burst is the attack's only §6 read; marking every link makes the chain shape legible. Divergence from the projectile puff's damage-landed gate is deliberate and documented.
6. **Floored-to-0 hits occupy chain slots, get VFX, skip ApplyDamage.** ACCEPTED. "Floored at 0" is the spec's own math; skipping `ApplyDamage(0)` avoids receiver-side zero-damage event noise. Unreachable with any authored row (needs falloff ≥ Dmg and ≥4 targets); the negative-falloff data-smell warning covers the adjacent misauthoring case.
7. **DamageCauser = the tower itself (attribution upgrade vs. the projectile path).** ACCEPTED. Receivers' documented step-2 chain resolves the tower's team from the ITeamAgent causer, so the same-team gate actively backstops the enemy-only selection — strictly stronger safety than unattributable-and-apply, at zero cost since no projectile intermediary exists. EventInstigator stays null in practice (see NIT on :452). No behavior change for the projectile towers.
8. **Freeze = gate-skip, not timer-pause.** ACCEPTED. Literal reading of the spec ("ALL tower firing gates on !IsFrozen()") and the exact counterpart of Building.h's state-only design ("the tower fire path re-checks IsFrozen() every shot"). No re-arm bug: nothing touches FireTimerHandle on freeze/expiry. Worst case a tower resumes up to one cadence tick after expiry — deterministic, and FrostNova (4 s) vs 1.5 s cadence guarantees ≥2 skipped shots, so the freeze is always visible.
9. **Match-end precedence needs nothing tower-side.** ACCEPTED, with one factual correction now that 099 is on disk: the invariant as worded ("the fire loop is still the ONLY timer a tower ever arms") is stale — ABuilding::ApplyFreeze arms `SpellFreezeTimerHandle` on the same object. The MECHANISM still holds, and is actually strengthened: `ClearAllTimersForObject` (SiegeGameMode.cpp:281) sweeps both handles, Building.h:100-107 documents that a match-end-silenced tower's spell freeze never expires, and even a hypothetical post-match ApplyFreeze expiry only flips a flag and resumes nothing (the fire loop is gone and OnStatsLoaded is single-fire). Tower.cpp's own contribution to the invariant — FireTimerHandle is the only timer *this file* arms, armed once — is intact. The stale SiegeGameMode.cpp comment is the WARN above (carry-forward; correctly not edited by either task under ruling 14 file discipline).
10. **Shadow-scan self-check.** VERIFIED independently (see verification item 10). The programmer's local list matches what is on disk; zero collisions. PASS.
