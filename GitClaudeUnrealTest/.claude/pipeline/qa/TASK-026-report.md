## Findings

1. **[WARN — cross-task carry-forward, not a TASK-026 code change]** Projectile.cpp (class-level) — in-flight projectiles are outside the match-end freeze and PlayAgain contracts. TASK-024's freeze (FreezeAI on units, PauseIncome, StopClock) and PlayAgain v2 (destroy ABuildings + resets) say nothing about live `AProjectile`s: one in flight at match end lands its damage after the Victory screen (bounded — single-target, ≤5 s lifespan), and one in flight across a PlayAgain reset can hit a freshly reset castle for stale damage. Suggested fix in TASK-024: on match end AND PlayAgain, `TActorIterator<AProjectile>` → `Destroy()`. Verify at TASK-040 M2b PIE. Not fixable inside this task's file scope (SiegeGameMode belongs to TASK-024's wave).
2. **[NIT]** Projectile.cpp:58-63, 136, 148-161, 73-82 — each spawn performs up to 5 soft-ref resolutions (OnConstruction: mesh+material; InitProjectile→ApplyTeamVisuals again: mesh+material; BeginPlay: NS_Damage). All are resident-asset `FindObject` lookups in practice (hero/units keep NS_Damage loaded; engine sphere + MIs load once) — same accepted pattern as the TASK-002 NIT — but projectiles spawn at volley rates. If projectile counts grow (M4 Longbowman, multiple towers), promote resolution to a cached-once static/CDO pattern. No action now.
3. **[NIT]** Castle.h:77-78 + handoff decision-4 wording — "returns the SCALED amount the castle actually took" / "the HP actually removed": on overkill (castle at 5 HP takes a 20 melee hit) the return is the full scaled damage (20), not the clamped HP delta (5). This is exactly M1's behavior (M1 returned `ActualDamage` regardless of clamp) — no regression, callers only test `> 0` — but the doc phrasing slightly over-promises. Cosmetic.
4. **[NIT]** Projectile.cpp:271-294 — now the THIRD hand-mirror of the closest-point-distance pattern (hero melee inline at HeroCharacter.cpp:204-210, `ASummonedUnit::GetDistanceToTarget` at SummonedUnit.cpp:734-757, this one). Mirror verified semantically identical (null → float-max; `ActorGetDistanceToCollision` on ECC_Pawn; `< 0` → actor-origin fallback for distance AND point). Accepted per the handoff's file-ownership rationale and the TASK-004 TryGetDamageTeam precedent; consolidate into one shared static when SummonedUnit.h ownership next frees up (post TASK-028).
5. **[NIT]** Castle.cpp:142 — `Super::TakeDamage` broadcasts the engine's `OnTakeAnyDamage` with the PRE-scale amount while castle HP drops by the scaled amount. No current listener exists and melee is unaffected; documenting so nobody later binds a BP to castle damage delegates expecting §3.0-scaled numbers there. The authoritative scaled surface is `OnCastleHPChanged` + the return value.

## Flagged-decision rulings (all 10, by handoff number)

1. **AProjectile does NOT implement ITeamAgent — PASS.** Verified against the live acquisition code: `ASummonedUnit::AcquireTarget` collects `GetAllActorsWithInterface(UTeamAgent)` (SummonedUnit.cpp:273) and `IsTargetAlive` treats unknown ITeamAgent types as alive (:724-725) — an ITeamAgent projectile would enter the non-pawn target bucket and be attacked by enemy units; hero melee (HeroCharacter.cpp:176) would strike it too. Team-as-plain-property + instigator plumbing + impact-time gate covers attribution without polluting targeting. CONSTRAINT recorded: any future "shoot down projectiles" feature must NOT be built by simply adding ITeamAgent — IsTargetAlive and acquisition need explicit handling first.
2. **Distance-test impact, no physics — ACCEPTED (blockout tier).** House pattern (TASK-003/004), and the anti-overshoot term is mathematically sound: `ReachDistance = max(ImpactRadius, Step)` means any tick whose step could fly past the target/aim point triggers impact/expiry first — no oscillation, no fly-through of the intended target at low FPS. ECC_Pawn works against every current and contracted receiver (pawn capsules, castle BlockAll, TASK-027 buildings block Pawns per spec). Accepted consequences (flies through world geometry, castle impacts at walls with closest point doubling as VFX point) match the documented blockout intent.
3. **Projectile-side same-team gate at impact — PASS.** Native `Cast<ITeamAgent>` valid per the TASK-002 ruling (`UTeamAgent` is `NotBlueprintable`, TeamId.h:25). Correction of emphasis vs the handoff: for non-pawn (tower) shooters this gate is the ONLY friendly-fire protection (receivers resolve tower shots as unattributable-applies), so it is load-bearing, not just belt-and-braces — it is correctly placed before `ApplyDamage` and expires without damage or VFX. Gate-at-impact (not at init) is fine: a mis-fired projectile flies its path and dies harmlessly, warned.
4. **TakeDamage returns the SCALED amount — PASS.** Full caller audit above (regression section): both consumers test `> 0` only; melee returns bit-identical to M1. Returning the scaled amount is also the correct reading of the AActor contract ("damage actually applied"). See NIT-3 for the overkill-wording nuance (behavior M1-identical).
5. **Harmless expiries spawn no VFX — PASS.** `DamageApplied > 0.f` gate before the puff (Projectile.cpp:262-265) mirrors the QA-approved TASK-016/020 return-value pattern; lost-target expiry, lifetime expiry, same-team gate, un-init expiry, and zeroed hits all despawn silently. Spec's "§3.8 telegraph baseline" reads as telegraphing HITS — a puff on a zeroed impact would be a false telegraph.
6. **Destroyed-but-extant targets flown to, not dropped — ACCEPTED.** A destroyed castle / dead-hidden hero stays `IsValid`; their TakeDamage guards zero the hit → no puff, silent despawn. Avoids duplicating three liveness APIs in a second class. Note: a destroyed castle has collision disabled, so `ActorGetDistanceToCollision` returns −1 and the flight resolves to the actor origin — the projectile flies into the hidden castle's center and expires. Invisible and harmless.
7. **`InitialLifeSpan = 5.f` — PASS.** Engine-managed (applied at AActor BeginPlay, works for deferred spawn), Epic's own FPS-template projectile pattern, nothing to clear on Destroy. Meets the spec's "~5 s safety" exactly.
8. **`USiegeDamageType_Melee` declared but unused — PASS.** Spec item (1) requires both classes; CONVENTIONS says "melee needs no tag" and the M1 byte-identical rule forbids touching the existing melee call sites — verified both still pass `UDamageType::StaticClass()`. Registry completeness, not dead code.
9. **Scaling via `IsChildOf` — PASS.** Subclasses of `USiegeDamageType_Projectile` inherit 50% automatically (self-match included). CONSTRAINT for M5: spell damage types must be declared as their own registry entries per CONVENTIONS, NOT as subclasses of `_Projectile` — the 50% coincidence must stay an explicit rule, not an inheritance accident.
10. **Hand-mirrored `GetDistanceToTarget` — ACCEPTED.** Verified semantically identical to `ASummonedUnit::GetDistanceToTarget` line-for-line (SummonedUnit.cpp:734-757 vs Projectile.cpp:271-294), including the origin fallback writing BOTH the distance and the out-point. Same precedent as the TryGetInstigatorTeam/TryGetDamageTeam mirror (TASK-004). Consolidation is NIT-4, post-TASK-028.
