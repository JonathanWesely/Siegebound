3. **[600] Seed-copy under `SlotNameOverride` writes fresh-GUID profile slots (the reported crumb). — ACCEPTED, no suppression.** TASK-604's `FAccountScratchGuard` tracks every created suffix and deletes both copies in its destructor (verified at `SiegeAccountTest.cpp:108-122`; all seven successful creates across the suite call `TrackActiveProfile`), so the two blind-authored sides already compose cleanly. Residual: only a mid-test crash could leak a scratch file, and the suffix is a fresh GUID that can never collide with player data.
