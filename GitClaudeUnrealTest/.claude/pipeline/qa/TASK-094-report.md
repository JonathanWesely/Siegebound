## Flagged-decision rulings (all 5, by handoff number)

   - Segment coverage `MyLocation → ProposedLocation` spans the full per-tick travel (25–50 uu at 1500 u/s @ 60/30 fps), so a 50–70 uu trunk hull cannot be stepped over at any frame rate — the no-tunneling claim holds, including the start-penetrating case (decision 5).

Not blocker-capable either way.
   - `ECC_WorldDynamic` inclusion is harmless-defensive: only tag-carrying actors can ever match, and only TASK-095 assigns tags. Accepted rationale (Movable-mobility conforms).
   - O(hit-result) per tick honored: no `TActorIterator`, no world scan (cpp:408-430).
2. **VFX puff on terrain death (deviation from the damage-landed telegraph gate) — ACCEPTED.** The spec itself sanctions exactly this: "impact VFX optional only if a one-line reuse of the existing impact effect" — and it is one line plus a null guard (cpp:261-264), null-safe, on the already-cached `CachedImpactEffect`. The qa/TASK-026 ruling-5 telegraph purity ("puff ⇒ damage landed") is hereby REFINED, not overturned: it continues to bind for impacts ON A TARGET (the gate at cpp:338 is untouched); environmental deaths on hillsides/trunks are spatially unambiguous and the readability of ruling 2 ("hills and trunks BLOCK projectiles") outweighs purity — a silently vanishing arrow reads as a bug. Precedent: TASK-056's unconditional blast puff already broke strict purity for a spec-sanctioned reason. Revert path recorded and verified: deleting the 4-line `if (CachedImpactEffect)` block yields the spec's silent-despawn alternative with no other change. See WARN 2 for the M7 note.
3. **Explicit nearest-hit min-scan instead of trusting engine hit ordering — PASS.** Belt-and-braces at negligible cost; `Hit.Time` is valid on touch-class and start-penetrating hits alike (Time 0). Tie at equal Time keeps the first-encountered hit — deterministic per trace, cosmetic-only consequence (which actor's ImpactPoint hosts the puff), exactly as the handoff states.
4. **`bTraceComplex = false` — PASS, with a binding cross-task carry.** Matches the art contract on both sides: CONVENTIONS mandates Use-Complex-Collision-As-Simple on ALL walkable terrain (simple queries answered by the tri-mesh — hills resolve) and FOOTPRINT-ONLY hulls on obstacles (canopy has NO simple collision ⇒ canopy shots pass, ruling 6). TASK-091/092 acceptance already requires readback verification of exactly these two properties (`CollisionTraceFlag`, `BodySetup_0.AggGeom`) — that verification is now LOAD-BEARING for this code; TASK-096 must treat a missing complex-as-simple flag on terrain as a projectile-law failure, not just an art nit.
5. **Start-penetrating hits honored (distance-0 death) — ACCEPTED.** Correct and required for the no-tunneling guarantee (a tick ending flush against a trunk must die next tick, not step through). Side effect (shooter on a hill firing steeply downhill can clip the crown lip at the muzzle) is physical high ground per ruling 2 — carried as a playtest WATCH (WARN 1 scope).
