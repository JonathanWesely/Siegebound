## Rulings on handoff-flagged decisions

1. **Castle approach: `bStopOnOverlap = false` + acceptance 50 + `bAllowPartialPath = true` (cpp:400-401) — APPROVED.** The correctness of Attack engagement is independent of path-following reach semantics: `UpdateState` flips to Attack purely on the closest-point-on-collision distance check (cpp:218), and `EnterAdvance` only uses move status to throttle re-pathing (cpp:388). The castle origin sits unreachable inside its own BlockAll, nav-carving geometry, so the partial path terminates at the nav edge flush to the wall — well inside Range 120 by closest-point measure — whereas an overlap-reach move to an interior point could either stop short or never complete. Degenerate case also verified: with SM_Castle not yet imported (no collision, no nav carve), the unit walks to within 50 of the origin and the origin-distance fallback (cpp:614-615) still triggers Attack. Residual behavior when a broken navmesh strands the unit out of range: a cheap re-request every 0.25 s (status Idle path, cpp:388) with the warning throttled to goal changes — acceptable, and it doubles as the retry the handoff describes.
2. **`AHeroCharacter::IsDead()` cross-dependency (cpp:584-586) — VERIFIED.** TASK-003 is qa-passed; HeroCharacter.h:102 declares exactly `bool IsDead() const` (public, BlueprintPure). The call site invokes it on a `const AHeroCharacter*` — const-correct, no rename occurred. Dead heroes (hidden, not destroyed per TASK-003) are correctly excluded from acquisition and attack.
3. **Nearest-target re-pick flip-flop (cpp:196-202) — ACCEPTED as spec-conformant, not a defect.** GDD §3.8 says "acquire **nearest** enemy within 600" and its v1-change note explicitly optimizes against kiting, which strict nearest-re-evaluation serves. The only tie-break the GDD defines (pawn vs building within 100) is implemented deterministically (cpp:290-298). Two exactly-equidistant pawns could alternate between checks, but the global `LastAttackTime` cadence gate (cpp:352-359) guarantees no extra hits and no lost cadence from any swap — flip-flop is purely cosmetic. If playtests show visible dithering, a sticky-target hysteresis is an M2 tuning task, not an M1 fix.
