## Findings

- [BLOCKER] SiegePlayerController.cpp:1628 (SpawnSpellReticle) — **RESOLVED in fix loop 1 (see bottom section).** Original finding: the reticle decal spawns DOUBLE-ROTATED and will not render as a ring on the ground. The code spawns `ADecalActor` with rotation `FRotator(-90, 0, 0)` on the belief (handoff flagged decision 6) that "the spawn rotation stomps the CDO root's relative rotation." **That claim is inverted for UE 5.8.** Verified in engine source: (a) `ADecalActor`'s constructor sets its root — the decal component itself — to `SetRelativeRotation(FRotator(-90, 0, 0))` (Engine/Private/DecalActor.cpp:30); (b) `FActorSpawnParameters::TransformScaleMethod` defaults to `ESpawnActorScaleMethod::MultiplyWithRoot` (Engine/Classes/Engine/World.h:457); (c) `AActor::PostSpawnInitialize` under MultiplyWithRoot COMPOSES the transforms — `FinalRootComponentTransform = RootTransform * UserSpawnTransform` ("Respect any non-default transform value that the root component may have received from the archetype", Engine/Private/Actor.cpp:4310-4324). CDO pitch −90 composed with spawn pitch −90 = pitch −180: the decal's projection axis (local X) ends up HORIZONTAL (world −X), not down. Consequence: on flat/hilly ground the ring texture smears along X and deferred-decal normal attenuation suppresses it on surfaces perpendicular to the projection axis — the reticle is invisible or a smeared streak, permanently (UpdateSpellReticle never touches rotation; SetActorLocation only). No crash, targeting logic still works, but the feature's one visual deliverable is broken and it fails the M5 exit criterion "reticle projects onto the ground surface." **Fix (one line + comment):** spawn with `FRotator::ZeroRotator` (identity composes with the CDO's −90 → −90, correct), and — to keep the programmer's explicitness goal — follow the spawn with `SpellReticleActor->SetActorRotation(FRotator(-90.f, 0.f, 0.f));` (SetActorRotation is ABSOLUTE world rotation, immune to CDO/spawn composition). Rewrite the cpp:1622-1627 comment: the engine composes, it does not stomp.
